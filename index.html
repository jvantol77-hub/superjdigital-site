Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Our games

Our games do not collect, store or share any personal information. They have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device, and deleting the game deletes them.

App stores

When you buy or download one of our games from Steam, the App Store or Google Play, that store handles your purchase under its own privacy policy. We only see summary sales numbers, not who you are.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Our web host may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.

Emails

If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Children

Our games are made for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not collect any from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Email superjdigital@gmail.com.
